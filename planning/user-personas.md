# User Personas

We aim to empower teachers by providing a simple way to measure and track
student engagement, enabling them to make informed decisions about their
courses.

Non-technical Junior developer Senior developer

## Persona 3: Private Tutor

Bio: Sarah is a 34-year-old private tutor who teaches English and Math to high
school students. She runs her own tutoring business and offers online sessions
via Zoom. She wants to improve student learning outcomes by making lessons more
engaging and tracking individual progress.

Needs/Goals: Sarah needs a simple way to measure student engagement during her
lessons, identify which topics students struggle with the most, and adjust her
teaching accordingly.

Frustrations: Traditional tutoring relies on student feedback and test results,
but Sarah finds it difficult to track real-time engagement. She is frustrated by
the lack of tools that give insights into how students interact with her digital
materials.

Scenario: Sarah wants to improve her teaching by analyzing how her students
engage with her online lessons. She visits the website looking for a tool that
tracks participation, quiz performance, and lesson completion rates. The
homepage should highlight real-time analytics and easy integration with online
teaching platforms.

## Persona 4: EdTech Entrepreneur

Bio: Mark is a 32-year-old entrepreneur building a new online learning platform.
He has a background in software development and is focused on creating an
engaging, data-driven learning experience. His startup is in early stages, and
he needs analytics tools to refine the product.

Needs/Goals: Mark needs a way to track student behavior on his
platform—identifying which lessons keep users engaged, where they drop off, and
how different content formats affect retention.

Frustrations: Most analytics tools are either too generic or too complex for an
early-stage startup. Mark struggles to find a solution that provides actionable
insights without requiring heavy development work.

Scenario: Mark is preparing to launch his platform’s beta version and wants to
implement analytics from day one. He visits the website to find an
easy-to-integrate solution that helps him optimize course content and user
experience. The homepage should emphasize plug-and-play analytics and support
for EdTech businesses.

## Persona 2: Teacher in Gaza

Bio: Amina is a 29-year-old high school teacher in Gaza who faces frequent
electricity and internet outages in her classroom. She is dedicated to ensuring
that all her students have a fair chance to succeed, even when disruptions
prevent them from completing online quizzes or lessons.

Needs/Goals: Amina needs a way to track which students dropped off due to
connectivity issues so she can give them another opportunity to complete their
work. She also wants to ensure that grades are based on what students were
actually able to answer rather than penalizing them for technical difficulties.

Frustrations: Most online learning platforms don’t account for mid-session
disconnections, making it unclear whether a student stopped engaging due to a
lack of effort or an internet outage. Amina is frustrated by the lack of
detailed drop-off tracking that could help her make fair grading decisions.

Scenario: Amina visits the website looking for an analytics tool that shows
where each student dropped off in a quiz or lesson. She wants a way to
distinguish between those who completed the task and those who were cut off,
allowing her to offer make-up opportunities or adjust grades fairly. The
homepage should emphasize drop-off tracking and session recovery tools for
teachers in unreliable network environments.
