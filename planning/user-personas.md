# User Personas

We help teachers who is interested in adding an analytics tool to their website.

---

## Teacher In Gaza

- **Bio**: Sally is a teacher in Gaza teaching Math to high school students, due
  to the destroyed educational system in Gaza, so now teaching students online
  through a platform.
- **Needs/Goals**: An effective and proven tool for monitoring students who can
  access the platform.
- **Frustrations**: Difficulty identifying students who have a problem with the
  internet connection.
- **Scenario**: Sally searched the internet for an analytics tool to add to the
  platform but didn't find a friendly tool for teachers who didn't have
  experience with technology so she asked her coworker and recommended our tool.
